1. What the app is

Fun Facts Shorts ("the app") is a personal Windows desktop program. Its owner uses it to make short fun-fact videos and post them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. TikTok

The app connects to TikTok only after the account owner signs in on TikTok's own page and authorizes it. It uses that permission only to show which account is connected and to post the owner's own videos to that account. The owner can disconnect the app at any time, in the app or in TikTok's settings.

3. Content

The videos are made by the owner with the app. Photos used in them come from Wikimedia Commons and are credited in each post's caption. Videos are labeled as AI-generated where TikTok asks for it.
