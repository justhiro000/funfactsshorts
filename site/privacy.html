Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who this is about

Fun Facts Shorts ("the app") is a personal Windows desktop program used only by its owner, to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It does not collect information about anyone else.

What the app gets from TikTok

  • The connected account's display name, username and profile picture (scope user.info.basic), to show which account the app posts to.
  • Sign-in tokens, so it can post videos (scope video.publish), and the status of each post.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server other than TikTok's own, and it is never sold or shared.

How long

Until the owner disconnects TikTok in the app, which deletes the sign-in from the computer and asks TikTok to revoke the permission. Permission can also be removed in TikTok's settings at any time.

Cookies and tracking

The app and this website use no cookies, analytics or ads.

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
